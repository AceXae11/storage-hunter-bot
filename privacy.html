Privacy Policy

The bot may process Discord information necessary to operate, such as server IDs, channel IDs, message content in channels where the bot has access, and information required to send update notifications.

3. Storage

The bot does not intentionally sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The bot may access publicly available Storage Hunters update information from third-party websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

5. Data Removal

If you believe information associated with the bot should be removed, contact the server administrators.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the server administrators.
